How to Confirm Coverage Fast

The fastest way to confirm service coverage is to share your full project address through our contact form or by phone. We review the location together with project type, expected tile area, and access details so we can provide a realistic planning timeline. If your location is inside our active route schedule, we typically arrange the right next step quickly and confirm scope details before work begins.

When travel distance is higher, we still take projects if the scope and logistics fit. In those cases, we may group visits by zone and propose a schedule window that keeps your renovation progress predictable. This approach helps avoid rushed starts and keeps material planning aligned with the installation sequence.

What We Need Before Scheduling

To prepare an accurate tile installation plan, we ask for project type, approximate area, preferred material, and target completion timeline. Photos or short videos of the current condition help us identify prep requirements such as waterproofing, leveling, crack isolation, and trim transitions. This reduces guesswork and allows us to set expectations before site work begins.

For condo projects, include elevator booking rules and work-hour restrictions. For detached homes, note parking and material access. These details save time on installation days and improve schedule accuracy, especially for multi-day bathroom or floor projects.
